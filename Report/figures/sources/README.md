## September 2026 merge and schematic revision

The three published map/contour PDFs were retained unchanged during the merge.
The original incoming assembly script is archived verbatim in
`Incoming_Background_Builder.py.txt`; it uses Pillow and CairoSVG. That archive
records the original assembly route, rather than the current executable workflow.

The current `Report/scripts/build_sourced_background_figures.py` uses NumPy and
Matplotlib to regenerate only the three explanatory schematics. It writes the
PDFs under `Report/figures/` and matching editable SVGs under
`Report/figures/sources/schematics/`, with `--figures lensing ia models` selecting
the diagrams. The published contours and research-result plots
are not regenerated by that command.

The incoming schematics credited GPT-5.6 Sol assistance. The current revisions
were prepared with Codex assistance: ray geometry and anisotropic tidal arrows
were clarified; labels enlarged; mathematical notation aligned with the report;
and the reconstruction workflow made explicit. These are explanatory drawings,
not measured data or numerical ray-tracing outputs. No completed student review
is asserted. The student should revise and explain the final drawings using
`Section2_Figure_Guide.md`.
