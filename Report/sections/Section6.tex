\section{Summary and Future Prospects}
\label{sec:summary}

The comparison uses 100,000 surfaces generated by sampling the $N_\Theta$
shape parameters of the flexible IA response model. Each surface contains
$N_zN_k$ values over redshift and wavenumber. PCA supplies the linear
reference, while Conv1D and PCA-AE learn nonlinear representations at the
same latent dimensions $L\in\{2,4,6,8,10\}$.

On the held-out validation set, the reported three-layer Conv1D run recovers
$98.65\%$ of the variance at $L=2$, against $96.23\%$ for two-component PCA;
$99.92\%$ at $L=6$; and $99.997\%$ at $L=10$.  At equal $L$ the nonlinear
map is more accurate than PCA. From $L=6$ onward PCA-AE gives similar average
errors to Conv1D. At $L=6$, Conv1D also has a smaller maximum pointwise
relative error. These numbers are validation results from a single seed;
the test split is reserved for final evaluation.

The next step is to repeat the most promising configurations with several
seeds and then evaluate the reserved test set. In the longer term, the
model family should be connected more directly to physical
intrinsic-alignment calculations and survey requirements.
