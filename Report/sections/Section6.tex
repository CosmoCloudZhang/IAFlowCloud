\section{Summary and Future Prospects}
\label{sec:summary}

The comparison uses 100,000 surfaces from 13 sampled shape parameters, each
with 3,131 values over redshift and wavenumber.  PCA is the linear baseline.
The Conv1D autoencoder compresses the same surface through a bottleneck of
width $L\in\{2,4,6,8,10\}$.

On the held-out validation set, the latest three-layer Conv1D run recovers
$98.65\%$ of the variance at $L=2$, against $96.23\%$ for two-component PCA;
$99.92\%$ at $L=6$; and $99.997\%$ at $L=10$.  At equal $L$ the nonlinear
map is more accurate than PCA.  From $L=6$ onward PCA-AE matches Conv1D on
the average metrics, while Conv1D remains better in the extreme tail.  These
numbers are validation results from a single seed.  The test split has not
been read.

The next step is to repeat the most promising configurations with several
seeds and then evaluate the untouched test set.  In the longer term, the
model family should be connected more directly to physical
intrinsic-alignment calculations and survey requirements.
